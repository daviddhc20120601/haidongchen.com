## GPU AI Factory · Principal Engineer

**AI Factory Build-Out** | **HPC Networking & Cooling Commissioning** | **LLM Training Recipes** | **Singapore**

### Professional Summary

Principal engineer working on large-scale NVIDIA GPU AI factories — currently at Firmus, an NVIDIA Cloud Partner (NCP). My work spans the full stack of an AI factory: rack-scale architecture and NVLink domain design, InfiniBand and Spectrum-X fabric commissioning, direct-liquid-cooling loop bring-up, and the training and inference recipes that turn raw FLOPs into delivered tokens on Blackwell and Hopper silicon.

Before focusing on GPU infrastructure, I spent a decade across generative AI, cloud infrastructure, data engineering, cybersecurity, and digital payments — leading cross-functional teams of 40+ engineers and delivering measurable outcomes including 30% infrastructure cost reduction and large-scale product launches.

### Key Achievements

- **AI Factory Delivery**: Bring-up and acceptance of NVL72-class Blackwell rack systems, from cable plant through collective-performance sign-off
- **Performance Recipes**: Reusable FP8/NVFP4 training and inference recipes per GPU generation, shortening time-to-target-throughput on new silicon
- **Team Leadership**: Led a 40+ member technical team at Alibaba Group
- **Cost Optimization**: Reduced hosting costs by 30% through infrastructure optimization
- **Product Launch**: Built and released "Chat with PDF" (HaidongGPT) as an official Alibaba Cloud product
- **Enterprise Solutions**: Developed a data mid-platform for a world-leading automotive company at Capgemini
